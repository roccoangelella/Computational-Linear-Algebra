topics studied:
- pivot as the leading nonzero entry used to eliminate entries beneath it;
- row echelon form (REF): zero rows at bottom, pivots move rightward down the rows, zeros below each pivot;
- reduced row echelon form (RREF): REF plus pivots equal to one and zeros above as well as below each pivot;
- concrete augmented-matrix example;
- REF is sufficient for back substitution in square nonsingular systems;
- RREF makes pivot variables, free variables, consistency, and full solution structure directly visible.
