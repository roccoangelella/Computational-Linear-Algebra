topics studied:
- a zero pivot makes the multiplier formula impossible even when the matrix/system may still be nonsingular/solvable;
- row swaps can move a nonzero entry into the pivot position without changing the solution set;
- partial pivoting chooses a large-magnitude available entry in the current column and swaps that row into the pivot position;
- small pivots produce large multipliers and can amplify floating-point errors;
- algebraically equivalent elimination paths can have very different numerical stability.
