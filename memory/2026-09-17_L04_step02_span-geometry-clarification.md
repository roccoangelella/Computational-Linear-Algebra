topics clarified:
- vectors in `R^2` live in the two-dimensional plane, but the span of a single nonzero vector is only a one-dimensional subset of that plane;
- `span{v}` is the set `{alpha v : alpha in R}`;
- for `v=(1,2)^T`, all multiples `(alpha,2alpha)^T` satisfy `y=2x`, hence lie on one line through the origin;
- dimensionality of the ambient space (`R^2`) is different from dimensionality of the subspace generated by a given set of vectors;
- two non-collinear vectors in `R^2` can span the whole plane, whereas two collinear vectors still span only a line.
