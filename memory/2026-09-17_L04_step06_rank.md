topics studied:
- rank defined as `dim(Im(A))`;
- practical interpretation of rank as the number of linearly independent column directions;
- examples of rank 1 and rank 2 in `R^2`;
- bound `rank(A) <= min(m,n)` for `A in R^{m x n}`;
- nullity as `dim(ker(A))`;
- rank-nullity theorem `rank(A) + dim(ker(A)) = n`;
- conceptual interpretation: input dimensions split into surviving output directions and directions collapsed into the kernel;
- full rank for square matrices and equivalences `rank(A)=n`, `ker(A)={0}`, and `Im(A)=R^n`.
