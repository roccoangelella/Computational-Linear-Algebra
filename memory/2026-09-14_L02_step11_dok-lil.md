topics studied:
- DOK (dictionary of keys) stores nonzero entries as mappings `(row,column) -> value`;
- DOK is convenient while entries are inserted or modified because the sparsity pattern can change cheaply;
- LIL (list of lists) stores each row as a list of nonzero column/value pairs;
- LIL is also convenient during incremental matrix construction;
- compressed formats such as CSR/CSC are generally preferable after the structure is fixed and repeated arithmetic begins;
- practical workflow: build in DOK/LIL, then convert to CSR for repeated row-wise arithmetic such as sparse matrix-vector multiplication;
- sparse-format choice depends on the computational phase rather than on a single universally best representation.
