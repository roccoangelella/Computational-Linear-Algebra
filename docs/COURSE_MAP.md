# Course map

This map is a retrieval-oriented reconstruction of the supplied A.Y. 2025/2026 corpus. Dates refer to transcript filenames. Use `docs/LECTURE_INDEX.md` for lecture-by-lecture navigation.

## Module 0 — Large-scale viewpoint and sparse matrices

**Why it exists.** A large-scale linear-algebra method is useful only if its storage and arithmetic scale. Sparse matrices contain many zeros, so the central computational idea is to store and process only nonzero entries while preserving matrix operations.

**Core topics.** Sparse/dense distinction; number of nonzeros and density/sparsity; storage schemes (coordinate/COO, compressed-row/CSR and related formats); sparse matrix-vector products; graph interpretations; ordering/permutation ideas; practical sparse operations.

**Primary sources.** `Slides/Lecture0921-SparseMatrices.pdf`; transcripts `01Lecture0924.txt`, `02Lecture0926.txt`; `Della Santa/LinAlgebra.ipynb` for SciPy sparse usage.

**Feeds into.** Iterative linear solvers, Krylov methods, graph methods and large eigenvalue problems.

## Module 1 — Python and computational linear-algebra tooling

**Core topics.** Jupyter/Python workflow; NumPy arrays, indexing/slicing and dtypes; dense and sparse linear-algebra primitives; SciPy sparse objects; basic reproducible lab workflow.

**Primary sources.** `Della Santa/IntroPython.ipynb`, `Della Santa/LinAlgebra.ipynb`, `Della Santa/CLA4LSPpyslides2223.pdf`; transcript `03Lecture1001.txt`.

## Module 2 — Linear algebra foundations and direct methods

**Core topics.** Matrices and linear systems; vector spaces, bases and linear maps; determinant/rank; norms; triangular systems; elementary matrices; Gaussian elimination, row-echelon and reduced row-echelon forms; numerical tolerance; conditioning as sensitivity of a problem/solution to perturbations.

**Primary sources.** `Slides/Lecture1003-BasicTools.pdf`; transcripts `04Lecture1003_1.txt` through `08Lecture1010_1.txt`; `PyLab01_basictools.ipynb` and its solution.

**Conceptual role.** This is the common language required by every later module: convergence is stated in norms/eigenvalues, orthogonalization works in vector spaces, and iterative methods approximate linear-system solutions.

## Module 3 — Orthogonality, QR ideas, projectors and least squares

**Core topics.** Inner products and orthogonality; orthonormal bases; classical and modified Gram–Schmidt; Givens rotations; Householder reflections; orthogonal matrices; projection operators; orthogonal projection; least-squares geometry; QR-style solution logic; stability/performance comparison of orthogonalization procedures.

**Primary sources.** `Slides/Lecture1016-OrtProj.pdf`; transcripts `09Lecture1010_2.txt` through `14Lecture1017_2.txt`, plus `17Lecture1024_1.txt`; `PyLab02_orthog.ipynb` and solution.

**Feeds into.** Least squares, Arnoldi/Lanczos, QR eigensolvers, SVD, pseudoinverse and PCA.

## Module 4 — Stationary iterative methods for linear systems

**Target problem.** Solve `Ax=b` when direct factorization is too expensive or destroys useful sparse structure.

**Core topics.** Fixed-point iteration; matrix splitting; iteration matrix; Jacobi and Gauss–Seidel; convergence via spectral radius; norm-based sufficient tests; stopping criteria; residual versus error; effect of condition number; computational comparison with direct methods.

**Primary sources.** `Slides/Lecture1019-Iterative.pdf`; transcripts `15Lecture1020_1.txt`, `16Lecture1020_2.txt`, `18Lecture1024_2.txt`, and the near-duplicate `19Lecture1027.txt`/`20Lecture1029.txt`; Saad is the main supplied deep reference.

**Important warning.** A small residual means `Ax` is close to `b`; it does not by itself guarantee a small forward error `x-x_*` when `A` is ill-conditioned.

## Module 5 — Data/function approximation and least squares

**Core topics.** Interpolation versus approximation/regression; polynomial models; overdetermined systems; least-squares minimization; normal-equation viewpoint; QR-based solution ideas.

**Primary sources.** `Slides/Lecture1024-Approssimazione.pdf`; transcript `21Lecture1031_1.txt` and the beginning of `22Lecture1031_2.txt`.

**Feeds into.** Krylov projection/minimization viewpoints and the later SVD pseudoinverse treatment of least squares.

## Module 6 — Krylov subspaces and projection methods

**Key definition.** A Krylov subspace generated by `A` and `b` is `K_m(A,b)=span{b,Ab,...,A^{m-1}b}`. It is a structured approximation space built from repeated matrix-vector products, which is attractive when `A` is large and sparse.

**Core topics.** Minimal polynomial; nested Krylov spaces; Arnoldi orthogonalization; Hessenberg relation; projected linear systems; Full Orthogonalization Method (FOM); residual minimization/orthogonality viewpoints; GMRES connection; finite-dimensional termination in exact arithmetic; practical early stopping.

**Primary sources.** `Slides/Lecture1030-Krylov.pdf`; transcripts `22Lecture1031_2.txt` through the first part of `25Lecture1107.txt`; `PyLab03_krylov.ipynb` and solution; Saad reference.

**Feeds into.** Lanczos and large-scale eigenvalue computation.

## Module 7 — Spectral clustering and graph Laplacians

**Core topics.** Graph clustering; adjacency/degree/Laplacian matrices; spectral information and connectedness; using Laplacian eigenvectors to construct a representation in which clusters become separable; relation to k-means.

**Primary sources.** `Slides/Lecture1106-SpectralClustering.pdf`; latter part of `25Lecture1107.txt`; supplied references by von Luxburg, Jiang, Ling, and Spielman.

**Conceptual role.** Demonstrates that an eigenproblem can reveal combinatorial structure in a graph.

## Module 8 — Eigenvalue algorithms I: power methods, shifts and deflation

**Core topics.** Power iteration for a dominant eigenpair; convergence controlled by eigenvalue magnitude separation and starting-vector components; inverse power method; shifted inverse iteration; Rayleigh-quotient ideas; using already-computed eigenpairs; deflation, especially for symmetric matrices.

**Primary sources.** `Slides/EigenValues/1 - Power Methods.pdf`, `2 - Shifting, deflation.pdf`, `lab-deflation.pdf`; transcripts `26Lecture1114.txt` through `28Lecture1119.txt`.

**Applications.** PageRank is a dominant-eigenvector problem; iterative eigenvalue ideas also underpin later spectral methods.

## Module 9 — PCA and k-means

**PCA goal.** Replace correlated/high-dimensional features by orthogonal directions that capture as much variance as possible, enabling dimensionality reduction and interpretation.

**Core topics.** Statistical recap (mean, variance, covariance/correlation); covariance matrix; principal components as eigenvectors/directions of maximal variance; scores/loadings; explained and cumulative explained variance; scaling/standardization; visualization and interpretation; relation to SVD; k-means; centroid interpretation; internal/external cluster evaluation.

**Primary sources.** `Della Santa/PCA_CLA4LSPslides_2526.pdf`; transcripts `29Lecture1121.txt` through `34Lecture1203.txt`; `PyLab04a`, `PyLab04b`, `PyLab05` and the homework-specific files.

**Assessment role.** One of the two mandatory projects.

## Module 10 — Eigenvalue algorithms II: localization, Lanczos and QR

**Core topics.** Gershgorin circles/disks for eigenvalue localization; limitations/interpretation; Lanczos as the symmetric specialization of Krylov/Arnoldi ideas, producing a tridiagonal projected problem; extremal-eigenvalue/Rayleigh-quotient connections; QR iteration for computing many/all eigenvalues; similarity transformations and convergence toward triangular/Schur-like form.

**Primary sources.** `Slides/EigenValues/3 - Gerschgorin circles.pdf`, `4 - Lanczos.pdf`, `5 - QR method for eigenvalues.pdf`; transcripts `35Lecture1205.txt` through the start of `38Lecture1215.txt`.

## Module 11 — Singular value decomposition and its applications

**Key factorization.** For `A in C^{m x n}`, the SVD writes `A = U Sigma V^*`, with orthonormal left/right singular vectors and nonnegative singular values on the diagonal of `Sigma`.

**Core topics.** Relation of singular values/vectors to eigenproblems for `A^*A`/`AA^*`; rank and norms; computation via reductions/eigenvalue methods; Householder transformations; optimal low-rank approximation; compression; least-squares solution through the SVD; Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse; minimum-norm solutions.

**Primary sources.** `Slides/EigenValues/6 - Singular value decomposition.pdf`; transcripts `38Lecture1215.txt` through `42Lecture0107.txt`.

**Feeds into / closes the loop.** Gives a stable global language for rank, compression, least squares and PCA.
